About NumberKundli

An independent, free mobile-numerology website — who runs it, how the analysis works, and what it does and does not claim.

Who runs it

NumberKundli is built and maintained by Deepam Mishra, working independently. It is a personal project, not a company product: there is no numerology school, teacher, brand or institution behind it, and it is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any of them. The teachings it summarises are credited on every page so you know where the method comes from.

What the site does

You enter a date of birth and (optionally) a 10-digit Indian mobile number. Everything is computed in your browser — nothing you type is uploaded — and you get:

  • your Birth number (day of birth reduced to 1–9) and Destiny number (full date reduced), with their planets and friendly/enemy numbers;
  • a position-by-position reading of all 10 digits of your mobile number, a 0–100 score and a plain-language list of warnings and strengths;
  • a Lo Shu grid showing your missing numbers, and lucky numbers, PIN and password suggestions that add them back;
  • wallpaper, phone-cover colour, ringtone and mantra suggestions for your numbers;
  • a PDF report you can keep, in English, Hindi, Marathi, Tamil or Gujarati.

Five stand-alone calculators (Life Path, Name Numerology, Compatibility, Personal Year, Lo Shu Grid), nine birth-number guides and a blog cover the same system in more depth.

How the analysis works

The engine is rule-based, not opinion-based. Every rule — which digit is good, bad or neutral at each of the 10 positions, which numbers are friendly or enemy to each Birth number, how the score is weighted — is written down as data and applied the same way to every number. When a warning appears, the card tells you exactly which digit at which position triggered it, so you can check the reasoning yourself. The same rules generate the lucky numbers and PINs, so a number the generator suggests will always pass the analyser.

Languages

The site is written in English. The Hindi, Marathi, Tamil and Gujarati versions are translations of the English originals; if you spot a translation that reads wrongly, it is a translation slip, not a different rule.

What it does not claim

Numerology is a belief-based practice. NumberKundli offers it for guidance, reflection and entertainment, not as medical, legal, financial or relationship advice, and it makes no promises about outcomes. Health-related notes (for example "BP, skin or kidney issues") are the traditional readings of the system, not diagnoses — please see a doctor for health concerns. The site does not sell mobile numbers; the "Where to buy" section only links to third-party sellers we have no relationship with.

Privacy in one line

Your name, date of birth, mobile number, PIN and password never leave your device. Details are in the privacy policy.
