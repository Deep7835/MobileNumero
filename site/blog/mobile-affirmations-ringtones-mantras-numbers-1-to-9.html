Mobile Affirmations, Ringtones & Mantras for Numbers 1 to 9

Numbers are not only visual. Traditional practice adds a sound layer: an affirmation to say before you pick up the phone, a ringtone style that matches your number's planet, and the planet's beej mantra. Here is the complete list for numbers 1 to 9. Use your Birth number first; if your Destiny number differs, alternate.

1 — Sun

"Every positive action I take leads to greater and greater success." · "I communicate with confidence and wisdom."

Ringtone: powerful motivational speech, drum beats. Mantra: Om Suryay Namah.

2 — Moon

"I am willing to let go of my past and take the next step towards a better future." · "I speak with clarity and inner peace."

Ringtone: soulful flute, nature sounds. Mantra: Om Chandray Namah.

3 — Jupiter

"By eliminating interruptions and distractions, I easily maintain my focus and trust." · "I attract wisdom and meaningful connection."

Ringtone: temple bells, classical music. Mantra: Om Brum Bruhaspatey Namah.

4 — Rahu

"I accept and release everything in my life that is beyond my power to change." · "I am grounded, stable, and attract positive changes."

Ringtone: electronic beats, meditative or soft tone. Mantra: Om Ram Rahvey Namah.

5 — Mercury

"I can't do everything by myself. I accept the help of others who offer it on my journey to realising my goal." · "I express myself clearly and attract prosperity."

Ringtone: fast-paced beats, uplifting tunes. Mantra: Om Bum Budhay Namah.

6 — Venus

"I will make effective use of my time. I will concentrate on talking to people who further my journey towards my goals." · "I communicate with love and balance."

Ringtone: romantic or classical instrumental. Mantra: Om Shum Shukray Namah.

7 — Ketu

"My day is a perfect balance of solo reflection, meaningful friendship, and resolute purpose." · "I am connected, intuitive, and clear in my communication."

Ringtone: cosmic sound, Tibetan bells. Mantra: Om Ketveye Namah.

8 — Saturn

"There is no need to control. I trust that everything will happen as it is intended to." · "Every call brings me progress and stability."

Ringtone: deep chant, slow meditation beats. Mantra: Om Shum Shaneshcharay Namah.

9 — Mars

"As I realise my current dream, I immediately start work on the next one. All I do supports the larger vision I have for my life." · "I express myself with power and positivity."

Ringtone: drum beats, high-energy instruments. Mantra: Om Am Angarakay Namah.

How to practise

  1. Set the ringtone once — it works passively every time the phone rings.
  2. Say the affirmation silently while the phone rings, before you answer. It takes three seconds and re-frames the call.
  3. Chant the mantra 11 or 21 times in the morning; some people set it as the alarm tone.

Frequently asked questions

Should I use my Birth number or Destiny number affirmation?

Start with the Birth number, which reflects your nature. Use the Destiny number affirmation on days when you are working towards long-term goals.

Can I use these mantras without a guru?

The beej mantras listed are simple planetary salutations ("Om, salutations to the Sun") and are widely chanted without initiation. Pronounce them slowly and respectfully.

Does the ringtone volume or length matter?

Not in the traditional rules. Choose a version you enjoy hearing — the point is that the sound matches the planet's mood.
