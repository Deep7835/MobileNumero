A PIN is the most frequently repeated number in most people's lives. Mobile numerology treats every repetition as a small reinforcement of that number's energy, which makes the PIN a surprisingly powerful lever. The good news is that the rules are simple.

The four rules

  1. Use your missing numbers. Draw your Lo Shu grid and put the digits that are absent into the PIN. Typing them adds the energies your date of birth lacks.
  2. Make the total a friendly number. Add the digits and reduce to one digit; the result should be friendly to your Birth number.
  3. Include your lucky / money number in the total. For most people that means a total of 5 or 6, or the number that appears as "best" in their chart.
  4. Prefer a balancer. A balancer is a number friendly to both your Birth and Destiny numbers. A PIN whose total is a balancer works for both sides of your chart.

Two more things from the class: avoid your enemy digits inside the PIN, and expect the effect of a changed PIN in about 45 days.

A worked example

Birth number 2, Destiny number 9, missing numbers 3, 5, 6, 7, 8. Enemy digits are 4, 8 and 9, so 8 drops out of the pool even though it is missing. Balancer numbers are 1, 3 and 5. The generator produces 3567 (total 21 → 3, uses four missing numbers), 3677 (total 23 → 5) and 5567 (total 23 → 5) as top picks. All three use missing numbers, avoid enemies and total to a balancer.

A word about security

Numerology optimises for energy, not for guessability. Never reuse your banking PIN for your phone, never tell anyone your PIN, and remember that a pattern such as 1111 or 1234 is weak regardless of its total. Our PIN checker runs entirely in your browser and stores nothing.

Frequently asked questions

Can a PIN have 6 digits?

Yes. The same rules apply: use missing numbers, avoid enemy digits and make the total friendly. Our checker accepts 4–6 digits.

Should the PIN contain 0?

The grid has no box for 0 and the class warns against 0 in several mobile-number positions, so a PIN without 0 is preferred. The checker deducts slightly for a 0.

How long before a new PIN "works"?

The class states that the effect of a changed PIN or password is generally seen in about 45 days.
