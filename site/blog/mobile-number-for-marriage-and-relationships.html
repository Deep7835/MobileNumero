Mobile Number for Marriage & Relationships: Positions 6 and 7 Explained

People usually judge a phone number by its ending. For relationships, look at the middle: the 6th position governs marriage and the 7th governs married life. The class material is detailed about these two places, and the picture is clear — most digits carry a warning, and only one is actively good.

The ideal middle

Put together, the friendliest pattern for a couple is a 1 at the 6th position and a 6 at the 7th — the Sun followed by Venus. Venus (6) is the planet of love and harmony, and the 7th position is where it does the most good. If 1 is an enemy digit for you (Birth number 8), 0 or 3 at the 6th position is a quieter alternative.

Beyond the number

  • PIN: 5666 (total 5), 5667 (total 6) and 2577 (total 3) are the class picks for a good married life; 6775 and 1266 for love life; 1247, 3569, 2557 and 1455 for harmony.
  • Password: a total of 6 — GANESHJI or BANANA — for marriage and children.
  • Wallpaper: a couple's picture (number 2) or a family photo (number 6).
  • Cover: silicone or rubber, the "soft" covers listed for 2, 6 and 7, for people who want to soften a guarded nature.

Reading a couple's numbers together

Ideally each partner's number has a 6 at the 7th position and a total that is friendly to their own Birth number. Do not try to make one number serve both charts; the number belongs to the person who carries the phone. What you can do is avoid a total that is an enemy for your partner's Birth number — that keeps the two phones from working against each other.

Frequently asked questions

Is 6 good everywhere in a mobile number?

No. 6 is excellent at the 7th (relationship) and 10th (money) positions and good at the 8th for creative professionals, but at the 5th and 6th positions it is linked to BP, skin and kidney issues.

My number has 7 at the 7th position — should I change it?

The class links 7 at the 7th position to divorce and financial loss, one of the heaviest warnings in the system. Run the full analysis; if the total is also unfriendly, a change is worth considering.

Does a good middle fix a bad ending?

No. The positions are read independently. A 6 at the 7th helps married life while an 8 at the 10th still burdens finances.
