Mobile Number Me 8 Kyun Nahi Hona Chahiye (Shani ka Ank)

Agar aapne kisi numerologist se mobile number ke baare me poocha hai to ek baat zaroor suni hogi: number 8 par khatam nahi hona chahiye. Ye mobile numerology ka sabse zyada dohraya jane wala niyam hai. Par iske peeche vajah kya hai, aur kya 8 har jagah bura hai? Dono ka jawab yahan hai.

8 ka matlab: Shani

8 Shani ka ank hai. Shani ko numerology me bura nahi, balki sakht mana jata hai — wo mehnat maangta hai, der se phal deta hai, aur hisaab poora rakhta hai. Jin logon ka mulank ya bhagyank 8 hota hai wo aksar dheere badhte hain par bahut pakka badhte hain.

Dikkat tab hoti hai jab 8 aise sthaan par baith jaye jahan uska sakht swabhav aapke khilaf kaam karne lage.

Aakhri ank 8 kyun nahi

Mobile number ki 10vi position paisa aur aamdani se judi hai. Yahi wo jagah hai jahan number ka asar sabse zyada aarthik maamlon par padta hai. 8 is jagah par aarthik nuksaan, rukawat aur karz se joda jata hai — isliye paramparik niyam saaf kehta hai ki mobile number ke aakhir me 8 nahi hona chahiye.

Iska matlab ye nahi ki 8 wale sabhi log garib ho jate hain. Matlab ye hai ki paisa aane me rukawat aur der zyada mehsoos hoti hai — Shani ka asli swabhav yahi hai.

To 8 kahan chalta hai

Yahi wo hissa hai jo zyadatar log nahi batate. Niyam sirf aakhri ank ke liye hai, poore number ke liye nahi:

Position8 ka asar
1, 2, 3, 4Koi khaas nakaratmak niyam nahi
7Rishton me doori — bachna behtar
8Peeth aur haddi se judi dikkat
9Samaj me rutba — kai mamlon me theek
10Aarthik nuksaan — yahi sabse bhari niyam hai

Agar aapka mulank ya bhagyank hi 8 hai, to number ke beech me ek 8 hona aapke liye aam taur par dikkat nahi karta — wo aapka apna ank hai. Har position ka poora matlab yahan diya gaya hai.

88 ya 008 ho to

  • 88 aakhir me: niyam aakhri ank se juda hai, isliye 88 ka asar lagbhag utna hi hai jitna ek 8 ka. Dusra 8 9vi position par aata hai, jahan uska koi khaas nakaratmak niyam nahi hai.
  • 008 aakhir me: ye do vajah se bhari mana jata hai — aakhir me 8, aur saath me zero, jo kisi bhi position ko kamzor karta hai.
  • 8 se shuru: pehli position rawaiye se judi hai. Yahan 8 ka matlab hai gambhir aur zimmedar shuruaat — koi chetavni nahi.

Aapka number pehle se 8 par khatam hai to kya karein

Ghabrane ki zaroorat nahi. Teen raaste hain:

  1. Pehle poora number jaanchiye. Aakhri ank sirf ek niyam hai. Ho sakta hai baaki 9 position aur total aapke liye achhe hon. Free analyser me daal kar poora score dekh lijiye.
  2. Agar paisa hi mukhya chinta hai aur score bhi kam aa raha hai, to number badalne par sochiye — khaas kar business number. Chunne ka tareeka is page par hai.
  3. Number nahi badal sakte to baaki numbers theek kijiye. PIN aur password badalna free hai aur usi system ka hissa hai — lucky PIN aur password yahan ban jate hain.

Business number ke liye khaas dhyan

Dukaan, office ya jis number par payment aati hai — wahan ye niyam sabse zyada maayne rakhta hai, kyunki 10vi position seedhe aamdani se judi hai. Vyapar ke liye number chunne ka poora tareeka yahan diya hai.

Numerology ek vishwas par aadharit paddhati hai. Ise margdarshan aur manoranjan ke liye liya jaana chahiye, medical, kanooni ya aarthik salah ke roop me nahi.

Frequently asked questions

Kya 8 har jagah bura hai?

Nahi. Niyam khaas taur par aakhri ank ke liye hai, jo paise se judi position hai. 1 se 4 tak ki position par 8 ke liye koi khaas nakaratmak niyam nahi hai, aur 9vi position par ise kai mamlon me theek mana jata hai.

Mera mulank hi 8 hai, tab bhi number me 8 nahi rakhein?

Aakhri ank wala niyam phir bhi lagu rehta hai, par number ke beech me 8 hona aapke liye aam taur par dikkat nahi karta, kyunki wo aapka apna ank hai. Poora number jaanch kar faisla lijiye.

Mera number 88 par khatam hota hai, kya ye dugna bura hai?

Nahi. Niyam aakhri ank se juda hai, isliye asar lagbhag ek 8 jaisa hi hai. Dusra 8 9vi position par aata hai jahan uska koi khaas nakaratmak niyam nahi hai.

Kya sirf aakhri ank badalne se kaam ho jayega?

Mobile number me ek ank alag se nahi badla ja sakta — poora number badalna padta hai. Isliye pehle score dekhiye; agar sirf yahi ek chetavni hai aur baaki sab theek hai, to shayad badalne ki zaroorat na ho.

8 wala number lene se nuksaan ho hi jayega?

Numerology koi guarantee nahi deti. Ye ek paramparik maanyata hai jo kehti hai ki paisa aane me rukawat zyada mehsoos hoti hai. Ise margdarshan ki tarah lijiye, bhavishyavani ki tarah nahi.
