Colour is the fastest way to add a planet's energy to your daily life, and your phone is the object you touch most. The class profiles ten phone colours. Here is what each one says — and, using the friendly-number chart, who should embrace or avoid it.

Matching colour to your Birth number

A colour is supportive when its number is your Birth number or one of its friendly numbers, and better avoided when its number is an enemy. Some quick examples:

  • Birth number 1 (friends 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 9): almost every colour works; black (8) is the one to avoid.
  • Birth number 2 (friends 1, 3, 5): white, silver, gold, blue, yellow and green are supportive; red (9) and black (8) are enemies.
  • Birth number 8 (friends 3, 5, 6, 7): blue, green, purple and pink suit you; gold, yellow, red and white are less helpful, and even black — your own number — is an enemy of 8 in the chart.
  • Birth number 9 (friends 1, 2, 3, 5, 6): red is natural, gold and yellow add authority; black (8) is the enemy.

Colour by profession

The class also links colours to work: pink for beauty, fashion and healing; blue for mentors and counsellors; green for business and marketing; purple for intuitive and creative fields; yellow for educators and spiritual seekers; black for long-term planners.

If you already own the "wrong" colour

You do not need a new phone. A back cover in a supportive colour overrides the body colour for numerology purposes, which is why the class gives colour tips for plastic and metal covers. Combine a supportive cover colour with a wallpaper for your number and the phone as a whole is aligned.

Frequently asked questions

Is black phone colour unlucky?

Black carries Saturn (8) energy: excellent for discipline and planning, but heavy for people whose enemy number is 8 — Birth numbers 1, 2, 4, 8 and 9. For them a blue or green cover is a better daily companion.

Which phone colour is best for business?

Green (Mercury, communication and trade) is the classic choice; gold adds authority for those whose friendly numbers include 1 and 3.

Does a transparent cover change anything?

A transparent cover lets the phone's own colour show, so the body-colour reading applies.
