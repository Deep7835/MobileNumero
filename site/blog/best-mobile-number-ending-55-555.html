Best Mobile Number Ending: Why 55 and 555 Are Considered the Luckiest

Mobile Number·02 September 2026·2 min read·By Deepam Mishra

In this article

  2. What 5 does in the last three positions
  3. Where 5 should not sit
  4. What if you cannot get 555?

Ask a mobile numerologist which ending they would choose for a new number and you will almost always hear the same answer: 55 or 555. The Advance Mobile Numerology Class calls a number ending in 555 "one of the best endings", and our analyser awards it the highest pattern bonus. Here is the reasoning behind it.

5 is Mercury — communication, business and freedom

In Chaldean numerology the digit 5 belongs to Mercury, the planet of speech, trade, quick thinking and adaptability. Because a phone is literally a communication device, 5 is considered the most natural digit for it. 5 is also the only digit with no enemy numbers in the compatibility chart — it is friendly with 1, 2, 3, 5 and 6 and neutral with the rest — which makes it a safe choice regardless of your Birth or Destiny number.

What 5 does in the last three positions

  • 8th position (career & health): 5 supports career success and fame.
  • 9th position (public relations): 5 is "at its best" — it boosts visibility and network.
  • 10th position (wealth): again 5 is at its best, keeping money in motion.

Ending with 55 covers the two most result-oriented positions with a Mercury digit; ending with 555 covers all three.

Where 5 should not sit

5 is not universally good. At the 5th position it is linked to child-related issues, and at the 6th or 7th position to marital problems, because Mercury's restless energy unsettles the family zone. So the ideal pattern is 5 in the last digits and something calmer, such as 6, in the middle.

What if you cannot get 555?

Numbers ending in 555 are sold as VIP numbers and can be expensive. Good alternatives, in order of preference, are:

  1. Ending in 55 — nearly as strong.
  2. Ending in 6 — excellent for money, ideal for Venus-friendly people (Birth number 1, 5, 6, 7).
  3. Ending in 3 or 7 at the 9th–10th positions — opportunity, confidence and spiritual gains.
  4. A 9 at the 8th or 9th position for energy and public standing.

Whatever ending you choose, check that the number's single-digit total is friendly to your Birth and Destiny numbers — a great ending cannot fully rescue an enemy total.

Frequently asked questions

Is 5555 as the ending even better than 555?

Not necessarily. The benefit comes from 5 occupying the 8th, 9th and 10th positions. A 5 at the 7th position (married life) can actually create marital friction, so 555 is the sweet spot.

Is a number ending in 55 good for business?

5 is Mercury, the planet of trade and communication, so 55 at the end is one of the most recommended endings for business owners, salespeople and consultants.

My number ends in 55 but the total is 8 — is it still lucky?

The ending helps, but a total of 8 is an enemy number for Birth numbers 1, 2, 4, 8 and 9. Run the full analysis to see the net effect for your date of birth.
