Chaldean Numerology Password Guide: Pick a Word That Matches Your Goal

Mobile passwords may be 4–16 characters long, and in numerology those characters carry numbers. The Chaldean system assigns each letter a value from 1 to 8 (there is no 9, which is considered sacred). Add the values, reduce to a single digit, and you have the "total" of the password. The class teaches choosing a word whose total matches your life purpose.

Digits inside a password count as their own value. Example: GANESHJI = 3 + 1 + 5 + 5 + 3 + 5 + 1 + 1 = 24 → 2 + 4 = 6.

The "avoid 8" rule

If your Birth or Destiny number is 8, the class advises against a password whose total is 8, even for property matters — Saturn on Saturn is too heavy. Choose a 5 (money flow) or 6 (harmony) word instead.

Making it a real password

A single dictionary word is a weak password, so combine the numerology with basic security: add digits or symbols (they change the total, so re-check), use a different password per account, and turn on two-factor authentication. Frequently asked questions

Why is there no 9 in the Chaldean chart?

In the Chaldean tradition 9 is regarded as sacred and is not assigned to any letter. A password can still total 9 through addition, as HANUMAN does.

Do capital and small letters have different values?

No. The value depends only on the letter, not its case.

Do symbols count?

Symbols have no Chaldean value and are ignored in the total; digits count as their face value.
