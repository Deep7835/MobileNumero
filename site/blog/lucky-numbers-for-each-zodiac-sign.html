Lucky Numbers for Each Zodiac Sign (And the Catch)

Every zodiac sign has numbers traditionally associated with it. They come from the sign's ruling planet and its friends, which means they are not arbitrary — but they are also not personal, and that is the catch this page is about.

The primary number is simply the ruling planet's number. The others are the planets traditionally friendly to it.

The catch

A zodiac lucky number is shared by everyone born across a whole month. Roughly a twelfth of the planet is Leo, and all of them are told their number is 1. That is a very broad claim, and it is the main reason these lists should not be the basis of a real decision.

Your Birth and Destiny numbers are far more specific: they come from your exact date, not a month-long window. When the two disagree, the birth numbers win — every rule on this site is built on them. Work yours out with the Life Path calculator.

When both agree, it is worth something

Here is the useful case. If your sign's number is also friendly to both of your birth numbers — a balancer — then two separate systems are pointing at the same digit. That is a reasonable number to use deliberately: in a PIN, in a password total, or as the total of a mobile number. The zodiac calculator checks this overlap for you and lists the numbers both systems share.

What not to do with them

  • Do not play the lottery with them. No tradition claims predictive power over a random draw, and anyone selling that is selling a scam.
  • Do not override your birth numbers. A sign number shared with a hundred million people is weaker evidence than one derived from your own date.
  • Do not stack systems until something agrees. Checking sign, then rashi, then Chinese zodiac until one gives the answer you wanted is not a method.

Colours, while you are here

The same ruling-planet logic gives each sign a colour — red for Mars signs, green for Venus and Mercury, gold for Leo, dark blue for Saturn signs. If you want to apply that to something, the phone colour guide covers how the numerology side treats colour, which is rather more specific.

Frequently asked questions

What is my lucky number by zodiac sign?

It is the number of your sign's ruling planet — Leo 1, Cancer 2, Sagittarius and Pisces 3, Gemini and Virgo 5, Taurus and Libra 6, Capricorn and Aquarius 8, Aries and Scorpio 9. The table above lists the secondary numbers too.

Which is stronger, my zodiac lucky number or my birth number?

The birth number. It comes from your exact date rather than a month-wide range, and it is what every rule on this site uses.

Can I use my zodiac lucky number for the lottery?

No. Nothing in astrology or numerology claims to predict a random draw, and anyone who says otherwise is selling something.

Two signs have the same lucky number — does that mean we are compatible?

Not by itself. Shared rulership means a shared vocabulary, not a match. Compatibility in numerology is worked out from both people's birth and destiny numbers.
