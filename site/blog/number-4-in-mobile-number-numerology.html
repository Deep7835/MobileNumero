Number 4 in Your Mobile Number: Why Numerologists Say Avoid It

Mobile Number·04 September 2026·2 min read·By Deepam Mishra

Most digit rules in mobile numerology are about position — a digit is good in one place and bad in another. Number 4 is the exception. Advanced mobile numerology simply says: "Avoid 4 number in mobile phone." Our analyser deducts points for every 4 it finds, wherever it sits, and an extra penalty if it lands at the 6th position.

4 is Rahu — the shadow planet

4 is governed by Rahu, the north node of the Moon. Rahu is not a physical planet but a shadow point; it stands for the unconventional, the sudden and the confusing. It can bring flashes of brilliance and sudden rises, but also sudden falls, obsessions and unexpected obstacles. In a device you use hundreds of times a day, that unpredictability is exactly what you do not want.

4 at the 6th position: depression

The one position where 4 gets its own dedicated rule is the 6th, the marriage position. Here the tradition links it to a depressive tendency. Combined with the general "avoid 4" advice, a number with 4 in the middle deserves a close look.

4 as a compatibility problem

4 is an enemy number for Birth numbers 2, 4, 8 and 9, and friendly only with 1, 5, 6 and 7. If your date of birth gives you a 2, 8 or 9, every 4 in your number is doubly unwelcome: once by the position rule and once by the compatibility chart.

Does this mean people born on the 4th are doomed?

Not at all. Birth number 4 people (born on the 4th, 13th, 22nd or 31st) are innovative, disciplined and hard-working. The advice is about the mobile number, not the person. In fact Birth number 4 people benefit most from surrounding themselves with their friendly digits 1, 5, 6 and 7 — in the phone number, PIN and even the wallpaper (a bold "1" wallpaper is recommended for them).

Balancing a number that contains 4

  • Ensure the total of the number is friendly for your Birth and Destiny numbers.
  • Put your missing numbers (from the Lo Shu grid) in your PIN to add the energies the number lacks.
  • Use a light-blue solid wallpaper or a mountain scene, which is the recommended imagery for the 4 energy.
  • Choose a leather, hybrid or hard cover; these are listed as best for Birth number 4 and give grounding.

If the number carries two or more 4s and an unfriendly total, the cleanest remedy is a new number chosen with the position table in mind.

Frequently asked questions

Is 4 bad for everyone?

The rule is to avoid 4 in the mobile number for everyone. It is least harmful for Birth numbers 1, 5, 6 and 7, for whom 4 is a friendly number.

What about the number 44 or 444?

Repetition amplifies a digit. Since 4 is the digit to avoid, 44 or 444 in a mobile number is considered stronger Rahu influence, not lucky.

Which digit should replace 4?

It depends on position and your Birth number. In the last three positions 5, 6 and 3 are safest; in the family zone (positions 5–7), 6 at the 7th position is the only actively positive digit.
