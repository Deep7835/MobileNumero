Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

This policy explains what information the NumberKundli website ("we", "the site") does and does not collect, and how it is used. The short version: everything you type into the calculators stays in your browser.

1. Information you enter into the calculators

The name, date of birth, mobile number, PIN or password words you enter are processed entirely by JavaScript running on your own device. They are never transmitted to our servers or to any third party, and we cannot see them. When you close the page they are gone, unless you generate a PDF, which is created and saved on your device only.

2. Data stored on your device

We use your browser's localStorage (not cookies) to remember three preferences: your chosen language, your light/dark theme and your cookie-consent choice. These values never leave your device and you can clear them at any time through your browser settings.

3. Analytics (only with your consent)

If the site operator has enabled analytics, we ask for your consent through the banner shown on your first visit. Only after you click Accept do we load an analytics script (Google Analytics 4 with IP anonymisation, or Plausible, which is cookieless). Analytics tells us which pages are visited and roughly where visitors come from; it does not record what you type into the calculators. Declining has no effect on the site's functionality, and you can change your mind by clearing the site's storage in your browser.

4. Third-party services

  • Google Fonts — typefaces are loaded from fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com. Google may log the request (IP address, user agent) as described in Google's privacy policy.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) — the PDF library is downloaded from cdnjs.cloudflare.com the first time you click "Download PDF".
  • External links — the "Where to buy a number" section links to third-party websites that have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for their practices.

5. Server logs

Like almost every website, the hosting provider that serves these pages may keep standard access logs (IP address, requested URL, browser type, timestamp) for security and capacity purposes. These logs are not combined with anything you enter into the calculators.

6. Children

The site is not directed at children under 13 and we knowingly collect no personal data from anyone.

7. Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Preferences stored in your browser are under your control. If you have questions about this policy, contact the site operator using the details on the home page.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time; the date at the top shows the latest revision. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
